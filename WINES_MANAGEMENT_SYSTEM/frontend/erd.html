Normalization Verification (3NF)

1. First Normal Form (1NF)

Every column stores atomic, single-valued attributes. No repeating groups or arrays. Every table has a distinct Primary Key.

2. Second Normal Form (2NF)

Satisfies 1NF. All non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key, eliminating partial dependencies.

3. Third Normal Form (3NF)

Satisfies 2NF. No transitive functional dependencies exist: non-key attributes depend exclusively on primary keys. For instance, customer contact data is not duplicated in orders.

Academic Project Viva & Evaluation Notes

Transactions & ACID

Order placement operates inside a transactional boundary. Stock checks, line item insertion, and inventory deductions succeed atomically or rollback completely.

Triggers & DB Constraints

Database-level business rules enforce stock availability checks (SIGNAL SQLSTATE '45000') and automatic stock decrements directly within MySQL.

Indexes & Optimization

B-tree indexes on foreign keys, categories, prices, and order dates provide sub-millisecond query execution on analytical views and filters.
