Database Cardinalities & Referential Constraints
Entity Relationship Cardinality Foreign Key On Update On Delete Academic DBMS Purpose
SUPPLIER → WINE 1 : M (One-to-Many) wine.supplier_id CASCADE RESTRICT Prevents deleting a supplier if wines exist from that supplier.
CUSTOMER → ORDERS 1 : M (One-to-Many) orders.customer_id CASCADE RESTRICT Customer record cannot be deleted while sales order history exists.
ORDERS → ORDER_DETAILS 1 : M (One-to-Many) order_details.order_id CASCADE CASCADE Deleting an order automatically cleans up all associated order line items.
WINE → ORDER_DETAILS 1 : M (One-to-Many) order_details.wine_id CASCADE RESTRICT Wine cannot be removed from database if previously purchased in sales orders.
WINE → INVENTORY 1 : 1 (One-to-One) inventory.wine_id (UNIQUE) CASCADE CASCADE Ensures every wine has exactly one tracked inventory stock row.
